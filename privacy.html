Privacy Policy – EAPP Invoices

Last updated: 16 November 2026

EAPP Invoices ("the App") is an internal tool of EAPP IT SERVICES FZ LLC, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). It connects our own accounting software to our own Google account so that we can send our own invoices and read our own invoice register. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

The App asks for two Google permissions, and only for our own company Google account:

  • Send email (Gmail "send" permission): to email invoice PDFs from our own account to our own mailbox or to recipients approved by our staff. The App cannot read, list or delete any email.
  • Google Sheets: to read rows from our own invoice register sheet and to write back the status of each row, such as the invoice number and whether it was emailed.
  • Google Drive (file access limited to files the App itself creates): to store encrypted backup copies of our own accounting database in a folder in our own Google Drive. The App cannot see, open or change any other file in Drive.

What data is involved

  • Invoice details that we type into our register: customer name and address, description of services, hours, rates and amounts.
  • The invoice PDF that we generate from that information.
  • A connection token issued by Google, which allows the App to keep working without asking for a sign-in each time.

How we use and store it

  • We use the data only to create invoices, email them, and keep the register up to date.
  • The data and the connection token are stored on our own office server, in a file readable only by server administrators.
  • Backup copies of the accounting database are encrypted on our server before upload and are stored only in our own Google Drive account. Google cannot read them; the encryption passphrase is kept by us and is never uploaded. Old backup copies are deleted automatically after about 30 days.
  • The connection token is never placed in documents, logs, emails or web pages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. Invoice PDFs are sent only to the recipients our staff approve.

Separately from the Google data above, invoices for UAE customers may be passed to our accredited e-invoicing service provider and the UAE Federal Tax Authority as required by UAE law. This is not done through Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Invoice records are kept for the period required by UAE accounting and tax rules. The connection token can be deleted at any time by revoking the App's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the token file from our server. After access is revoked, the App can no longer reach the Google account.

Security

The App runs on our own office server. Access to the App, the server and the Google account is limited to authorised staff, each with an individual sign-in, and the App records sign-in attempts and locks accounts after repeated failures.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
